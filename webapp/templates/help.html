WARRANTY POLICY
14 days
Standard warranty on every account, starting the moment it's delivered. Covers the account being taken back, banned through no fault of yours, or login info stopping working.
7 days
If the authenticator app is removed from the account after delivery, warranty drops to 7 days from purchase — removing 2FA weakens the account's protection.
No warranty
If all authenticator backup codes and the recovery email are changed or removed, the account is no longer covered — we can no longer verify or recover it.
⚠️
Trade items (MM2, Blade Ball, Gamepass/Fruit) are delivered instantly and are not covered by warranty — check the item on arrival.
HOW TO GET YOUR CODE & INSTALL THE APP
  1. Install Google Authenticator or Authy from your phone's app store.
  2. Open your order history and copy the login code shown for your account — this refreshes every 30 seconds, so copy it right before you use it.
  3. When Roblox asks for a verification code at login, paste the current code shown on your order page.
  4. Do not delete or transfer the authenticator entry off our systems — doing so reduces your warranty to 7 days (see policy above).
  5. Still stuck? Message the admin directly — screen-share support is available for first-time buyers.
FAQ
How fast is delivery?

Most orders — account or trade — auto-deliver within 10 minutes of payment confirming on KHQR.

Can I buy from Telegram instead of the website?

Yes. Open the Store Bot, pick your item, and pay the same way. Orders placed in Telegram show up in the bot's own order history rather than the website's — they use separate accounts.

Do I need an account to buy on the website?

Yes — create one with a name, email, and password so we can attach your order history and let you reset your password if needed.

What happens if I lose my password?

Use "Forgot password" on the login page — we'll email a reset code to the address on your account.
